## Why one form and not a baseline plus an endline

Session 1 is a single online session of 45 to 60 minutes for a public audience. A separate
baseline form would cost about 3 minutes of a short session, many people join late and would
miss it, and the two forms would have to be matched person by person without collecting names.

So the form uses a **retrospective pre and post** design (sometimes called "then and now"):
after the session, people rate how well they could do each thing **before today**, then how
well they can do it **now**. The gap between the two is the self-reported gain.

- It needs only one form, so response rates stay higher.
- It avoids "response shift": before a session, beginners do not yet know what they do not
  know, so they often rate themselves too high on a baseline. Rating "before" after the
  session gives a more honest comparison.
- Three short knowledge questions add an objective check next to the self-ratings.
- Session 1 is theory, so the overall ratings ask about understanding the mechanics and
  confidence to explain them, not usefulness. An open "what made sense for the first time"
  question shows which ideas landed.

If you want evidence of real change in practice, add a 2-question follow-up 4 weeks later
(see the end of this file).

## The questions

**1. Before today, how often did you use AI tools such as ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini?**
Multiple choice, required.
Never / A few times in total / About once a week / Most days

**2. BEFORE this session, how well could you do each of these?**
Grid, required. Scale: 1 Not at all, 2 A little, 3 Somewhat, 4 Well, 5 Very well.
- Explain the difference between a chatbot, a workflow and an AI agent
- Describe the agent loop: plan, act, observe
- Explain why an agent needs tools, and who actually runs them
- Pick a planning habit that fits a job (think step by step, look it up as you go, to-do list first, check and fix)

**3. NOW, after this session, how well can you do each of these?**
Same grid, same rows, required.

**4. In an AI agent, who decides the next step?**
Multiple choice, required.
- A person writes every step in advance
- The model decides as it goes **(correct)**
- The computer picks a step at random
- Nobody: it always follows the same fixed script

**5. When an agent "uses a tool", what really happens?**
Multiple choice, required.
- The model does the work itself and creates the result
- The model asks for the tool, the computer runs it and sends the result back **(correct)**
- The tool takes over and replaces the model
- You have to run every tool by hand

**6. You ask an AI to plan an outdoor event for this Saturday, and it needs this week's weather forecast. Which habit helps most?**
Multiple choice, required.
- Think step by step
- Look it up as you go **(correct)**
- To-do list first
- Check and fix

**7. Overall, how much did this session help you understand how AI agents work?**
Linear scale 1 to 5, required. 1 Not at all, 5 A lot.
(Session 1 is theory, so this asks about understanding the mechanics, not usefulness.)

**8. How confident are you that you could explain to a friend how an AI agent works?**
Linear scale 1 to 5, required. 1 Not confident, 5 Very confident.

**9. What is one thing about how AI agents work that surprised you or made sense for the first time today?**
Paragraph, optional.

**10. What was unclear, or what should we improve?**
Paragraph, optional.

## How to read the results

| Measure | From | What to report |
|---------|------|----------------|
| Self-rated gain | Q2 and Q3 | Average "now" minus average "before", per row and overall (scale 1 to 5) |
| Knowledge | Q4 to Q6 | Percent correct per question, and percent who got all three right |
| Overall understanding | Q7 | Percent who chose 4 or 5 |
| Confidence to explain | Q8 | Percent who chose 4 or 5 |
| Ideas that landed | Q9 | Group answers by concept (levels of AI setups, the loop, tools, the four habits, staying in charge) and count each |
| Gains by experience | Q1 | Repeat the gain and knowledge results for "Never / A few times" vs "Weekly / Most days" |
