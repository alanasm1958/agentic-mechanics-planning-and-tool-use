# Summary: Corner Café sales, 1 September to 30 November 2026

All figures come from cafe_sales_clean.csv (946 rows, 89 trading days). Amounts are in US
dollars, as written in the till records. Refunds and the 4 smudged rows are not included
(see cleaning_notes.md).

- **Total revenue: $55,510.40** from 16,463 items sold, an average of $623.71 a day.
- **Top 5 products by revenue:**
  1. Cappuccino: $11,406.50 (3,259 cups, about 37 a day)
  2. Latte: $6,957.80
  3. Chicken Sandwich: $6,955.00
  4. Croissant: $5,650.00
  5. Soup of the Day: $4,970.00
- **Cappuccino alone brings in about one fifth (21%) of all revenue.** Latte and Chicken
  Sandwich are almost tied in second place, only $2.80 apart.
- **Busiest day of the week: Saturday**, with an average of $877.75 a day. Sunday is second
  ($744.48). Monday is the quietest ($481.26), so a typical Saturday brings in about 1.8 times
  as much as a typical Monday.
- **Month by month:** September $17,321.70, October $19,729.40, November $18,459.30.
- **The trend is up, then steady.** Average sales per trading day rose from $597.30 in
  September to $636.43 in October (up about 7%) and stayed level in November ($636.53).
  November's total is lower than October's only because it had fewer trading days (29 against 31).
- **Slowest seller: Matcha Latte**, added on 1 October. It sells about 1 cup a day and brought
  in $364.80, under 1% of revenue.
