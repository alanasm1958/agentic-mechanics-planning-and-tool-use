# Storyboard for Demo 2: Corner Café 30-second promo

Format: vertical video, 1080 by 1920, 30 seconds, 30 frames a second, no sound.
Five scenes of 6 seconds each, joined by short half-second cross-fades.
Warm palette: cream, terracotta, coffee brown, mustard and peach. Large rounded lettering.
All text sits in the middle of the screen, clear of the areas that phone apps cover with
buttons at the top and bottom.

Facts used (all from summary.md): Cappuccino is the number one product (3,259 cups in three
months); Latte, Chicken Sandwich and Croissant are next in the top four; Saturday is the
busiest day and Monday the quietest. From the brief: the name Corner Café and the opening
hours, 07:00 to 19:00 every day. No prices, offers or other claims.

| Scene | Time | Text on screen | Picture and movement |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Hello | 0 to 6 s | "Hello, neighbour!" then "Welcome to Corner Café" | Cream background. A big mustard sun rises from the bottom. Words fade and slide up one line at a time. |
| 2. Number one | 6 to 12 s | "Our number one" / "Cappuccino" / "3,259 cups" / "in three months" | Terracotta background. A simple white cup on a saucer with gently waving steam. The number counts up from 0 to 3,259. |
| 3. Favourites | 12 to 18 s | "Neighbourhood favourites" / "Latte" / "Chicken Sandwich" / "Croissant" | Cream background. Three rounded cards slide in from the right, one after another, each with a coloured dot. |
| 4. Busy days | 18 to 24 s | "Saturday is our busiest day" / "Prefer it quiet? Try a Monday." | Coffee-brown background. A row of seven day circles (M T W T F S S). The Saturday circle grows and turns mustard. |
| 5. Visit us | 24 to 30 s | "Corner Café" / "Open every day" / "07:00 to 19:00" / "See you soon!" | Mustard background with soft peach circles drifting. The name pops in, then the hours in a white pill. All text stays on screen for the last 2 seconds while the circles drift. |

Check plan: confirm the length with ffprobe (30 seconds, give or take 1), confirm the file
decodes from start to end, and pull one still frame from the middle of each scene to look at.
