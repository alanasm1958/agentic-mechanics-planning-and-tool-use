# Plan for Demo 1B: slide outline for cafe_review.pptx

Source: cafe_sales_clean.csv (946 rows, 1 September to 30 November 2026) and summary.md.
Every number below was recalculated from the clean file. Six slides, one idea each.

| # | Slide title (the message) | What is on it |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Corner Café: three months in numbers | Subtitle: sales review, September to November 2026, to help plan next month's menu and opening hours. Dark blue background. |
| 2 | Sales rose in October, then held steady | Three big numbers: **$55,510** total sales, **16,463** items sold, **$624** average per day. One small line under them: September $17,322, October $19,729, November $18,459, and a note that per day November ($637) matched October ($636). |
| 3 | Cappuccino brings in a fifth of all sales | **Chart 1:** horizontal bars, sales by product for all 11 products, highest first. Cappuccino ($11,407) in blue, others in soft grey. Short note: Latte and Chicken Sandwich are almost tied. |
| 4 | Weekends are busiest, Saturday most of all | **Chart 2:** columns, average sales per day for Monday to Sunday. Saturday ($878) and Sunday ($744) in blue. Note: Monday is quietest ($481). Averages are used because two Tuesdays have no records. |
| 5 | Matcha Latte has not caught on | Big number: **1.3 cups a day** since it was added on 1 October, $365 in total, under 1% of sales. **Chart 3:** average cups per day for each drink, Matcha Latte highlighted (Cappuccino 37, Latte 20.6). |
| 6 | Three things to try next month | 1. Never run out of the top three, especially at weekends. 2. Decide on Matcha Latte: drop it or give it one last month with a clear target. 3. Put extra staff on Saturday and Sunday, and record the time of each sale for a month before changing opening hours, because the records show days, not hours. Dark blue background. |

Design: white content slides, dark blue (#0063AC family) title and closing slides, Arial
throughout so the layout looks the same on any laptop. Blue is used only to highlight the
one thing each chart is about; everything else is soft grey. A small source line at the
bottom of each content slide. Charts are real PowerPoint charts, so the owner can click
them and see the numbers.
